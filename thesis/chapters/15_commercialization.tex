\section{Product Definition}

\section{Intellectual Property Strategy}

\subsection{Potential Patents}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dual-trigger rep segmentation:} Stillness + extremum detection for boundary identification.

    \item \textbf{NPN level-shifter sync circuit:} Cost-effective 1.8V-to-3.3V conversion.

\section{Regulatory Compliance}

\subsection{FCC Certification}

ESP32 radio requires FCC Part 15 certification:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Testing cost: \$5,000-10,000
    \item Timeline: 8-12 weeks
    \item Relaxation possible with pre-certified ESP32 module
\end{itemize}

\subsection{CE Marking (Europe)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item RED directive (radio equipment)
    \item RoHS compliance (lead-free)
    \item Testing cost: EUR 5,000-8,000
\end{itemize}

\subsection{FDA Considerations}

If marketed for rehabilitation or medical monitoring:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Class I device (general wellness exemption may apply)
    \item 510(k) premarket notification if medical claims made
    \item QSR compliance (quality system regulation)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Privacy}

\begin{itemize}
    \item GDPR compliance for EU customer data
    \item CCPA compliance for California residents
    \item Minimal data collection policy (anonymized analytics only)
\end{itemize}

\section{Risk Analysis}

\subsection{Technical Risks}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Supply chain:} Chip shortages delay production. Mitigation: multi-source critical components, hold safety stock.
    \item \textbf{Battery safety:} Thermal runaway rare but catastrophic. Mitigation: UL-certified cells, protective circuits.
    \item \textbf{Firmware bugs:} Post-release bugs require OTA updates. Mitigation: extensive beta testing, staged rollouts.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Market Risks}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Competitor price drop:} Established players undercut pricing. Mitigation: cost structure advantage, differentiation via open API.
    \item \textbf{Market slow growth:} VBT adoption slower than projected. Mitigation: diversify into rehabilitation/clinical market.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}

Commercial translation from research prototype to market-ready product requires careful planning across technical, regulatory, financial, and marketing dimensions. The estimated BOM cost of \$18 establishes a healthy margin at \$149 MSRP. Key success factors include:
\begin{itemize}
