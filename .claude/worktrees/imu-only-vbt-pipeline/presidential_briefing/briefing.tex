% --- THE VISION ----------------------------------------------------------
\sectionhead{The Vision}{}
{\fontsize{9pt}{12pt}\selectfont\color{darkText}\justifying
Project VBT-X1 is a faculty-led initiative to design, build, and clinically
validate a postage-stamp-sized intelligent sensor that measures human movement
with the precision of a hospital laboratory --- and the convenience of a
smart watch. Worn discreetly during training, physical therapy, or
post-surgical rehabilitation, it provides physicians and coaches with
real-time, objective data on muscular performance, fatigue, and recovery.
\par\vspace{4pt}
Every component --- from the sensor electronics to the custom silicon chip
at its heart --- is being designed and built by our students and faculty.
\par}
\vspace{6pt}

% --- CLINICAL & SOCIETAL IMPACT -----------------------------------------
\sectionhead{Impact}{Where VBT-X1 Changes Lives}
{\fontsize{9pt}{11.5pt}\selectfont\color{darkText}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt, itemsep=2pt, topsep=0pt]
\item[\iconHeart] \textbf{Sports Medicine.}~Objective fatigue and overtraining detection
for national-level athletes and Olympic candidates.
\item[\iconPlus] \textbf{Clinical Rehabilitation.}~Quantitative recovery
benchmarks after ACL, rotator-cuff, and orthopaedic surgery.
\item[\iconBar] \textbf{Public Health.}~Bringing laboratory-grade movement
assessment to gyms, clinics, and physiotherapy rooms nationwide.
\item[\iconChip] \textbf{National Technology Sovereignty.}~A locally
designed semiconductor product --- a first for our university.
\end{itemize}}
\vspace{6pt}

% --- WHY WE LEAD --------------------------------------------------------
\sectionhead{Why We Lead}{}
{\fontsize{9pt}{12pt}\selectfont\color{darkText}\justifying
Few institutions worldwide attempt this full vertical: sensors, embedded
software, machine intelligence, and custom microchip design --- all under
one academic roof. Our team has assembled the rarely-combined expertise to
take a clinical idea from the laboratory bench to a fabricated chip, with
international research partners observing our progress.\par}
